What the reported cases look like

Real cases come from patient contact with someone who has access. The targets are officials, military staff and technology executives. Every entry below links to its reporting, and allegations are labelled as allegations.

The pattern

Access, patience and personal contact. Nationality is not part of the pattern, and China's own security ministry warns its staff about honey traps too.

Count the flags, then ask what you hold. Four flags and a Wi-Fi password is a clingy partner. Two flags and an export licence is a phone call to compliance.

About · method and limits

How IdentiSpy scores you

Two scores

Part A has four questions on a 0 to 3 scale and becomes your Target value. Part B has six questions on the same scale and becomes the Suspicion index. Each is shown as a percentage of its maximum.

Nine verdicts

Each score is sorted into low, medium or high. The pair picks the stamp, from Clear to Code red. A low target with a high suspicion reads as nosy, because nobody budgets an operation against someone with nothing to take.

What it never does

It never asks where anyone is from, never stores answers, and never sends anything off this page. Your answers stay in your browser tab.

What it is

A joke built from ten multiple-choice answers. It is not intelligence work and it proves nothing about a real person. If something feels wrong, talk to your partner or your security team.
